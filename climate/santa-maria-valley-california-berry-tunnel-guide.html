☀️ Solar Radiation & Photobiology Optimization

In Santa Maria, California, USA, natural solar Daily Light Integral transitions from 18-24 mol/m²/day during winter solstice to 45-52 mol/m²/day at summer peak. For commercial crops like Substrate Table-Top Strawberry, Raspberry, Blackberry, target canopy DLI requires dynamic screen management to avoid photoinhibition during midday radiation spikes exceeding 850 W/m².

🛡️ Recommended Thermal & Shade Screen Specification

Screen Specification: High-PAR Polyethylene Film with Infrared Additives

Primary Cooling Strategy: Automated High-Tunnel Vent Deployment

Maintaining positive day-night temperature differential (DIF) and nighttime dehumidification minimizes condensation on cold structural glass surfaces, suppressing Botrytis and powdery mildew spore viability.
