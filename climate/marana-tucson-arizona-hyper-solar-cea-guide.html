☀️ Solar Radiation & Photobiology Optimization

In Marana and the greater Tucson Basin, Arizona, solar irradiance reaches globally elite levels, with outdoor Daily Light Integral exceeding 52-60 mol/m²/day in summer and maintaining a robust 24-30 mol/m²/day even during December solstice. The primary photobiological mandate is managing excessive infrared heat gain while preserving photosynthetically active radiation (PAR). High diffuse light transmission screens (40-55% shade rating) with reflective aluminum strips prevent canopy leaf scorch and tip burn in vine crops.

🛡️ Recommended Evaporative Cooling & Screen Specification

Cooling Architecture: Cellulose Evaporative Pad-and-Fan System with High-Pressure Fogging (70 bar)

Screen Specification: Dual-layer retractable system: Upper aluminized open-structure reflective screen + Lower energy saving / diffuse screen

Due to the extremely dry desert ambient wet-bulb depression (often exceeding 15-20°C in May and June), evaporative cooling efficiency operates at peak thermodynamic performance, allowing interior greenhouse temperatures to remain 8-12°C below ambient outdoor maximums.

🌿 Dominant Commercial Production Crops

Leading protected cropping commodities in southern Arizona include: High-wire Beefsteak & Truss Tomatoes, Bell Peppers, Seedless Cucumbers, and Specialized Hydroponic Research Crops (CEAC Hub).
