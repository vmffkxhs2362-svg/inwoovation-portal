Canopy Light Extinction (Beer-Lambert Law) & Diffuse Glass Transmission

Published: 2026-09-01 • Author: Inwoo Hwang • Reading Time: 9 min

In high-wire greenhouse tomato and cucumber crops with high Leaf Area Index ($LAI = 3.5–4.5$), direct sunlight creates severe light saturation and thermal stress in the top leaves while starving middle and bottom foliage. Implementing diffuse greenhouse glass ($Hortiscatter \ge 65\%$) lowers the extinction coefficient ($k$) from $0.75$ to $0.55$, enabling deep photon penetration and boosting whole-canopy photosynthesis by up to $18\%$.

1. The Beer-Lambert Canopy Extinction Law

I(z) = I_0 \times \exp(-k \times LAI(z))

Where diffuse photons eliminate harsh shadows and drive efficient electron transport in lower canopy layers.
