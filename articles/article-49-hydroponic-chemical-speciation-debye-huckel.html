Hydroponic Chemical Speciation, Debye-Hückel Activity & Saturation Indices

Published: 2026-09-01 • Author: Inwoo Hwang • Reading Time: 9 min

In recirculating hydroponic fertigation, elevated pH ($>6.2$) or unbalanced $Ca^{2+}$ and $H_2PO_4^-$ dosing triggers severe precipitation of insoluble dicalcium phosphate ($CaHPO_4$) and calcite ($CaCO_3$), causing emitter blockage. We articulate the Debye-Hückel activity coefficient and Saturation Index ($SI = \log_{10}(IAP / K_{sp})$) thermodynamic framework.
