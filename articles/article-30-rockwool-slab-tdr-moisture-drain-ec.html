Rockwool Slab Hydrodynamics, TDR Moisture Sensors & Drainage EC Balancing

Published: 2026-09-01 • Author: Inwoo Hwang • Reading Time: 8 min

Hydrophilic stone wool (Rockwool) slabs provide an inert, pathogen-free rootzone with over 95% total pore volume. However, due to weak capillary matric suction ($\psi_m \approx -1 \text{ to } -5\text{ kPa}$), improper drainage scheduling causes rapid salt build-up (pore-water $EC > 5.5\text{ mS/cm}$) and root tip burn.

1. The Overnight Dry-Down Rule

Targeting an overnight moisture decline (dry-down) of $8\% \text{ to } 12\%$ stimulates generative flowering signals and forces air (oxygen) renewal into the slab interstitial pores before the first morning irrigation shot.
