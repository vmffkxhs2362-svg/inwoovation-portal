Greenhouse Envelope Peak Heat Loss Coefficients (U-Values) & Thermodynamic Thermal Screen Modeling

📅 2026-07-30 🏷️ Thermodynamics & Energy ✍️ Inwoovation Lab Research Team

1. Winter CEA Energy Economics: Nocturnal Envelope Heat Loss Dynamics

Heating energy represents up to 30–45% of total annual operational expenditure (OPEX) in commercial temperate and cold-climate greenhouses. At night, greenhouses function as uninsulated, high-surface-area thermodynamic radiators. Unlike traditional civil buildings with high thermal mass, protected agricultural structures feature thin glazing materials designed primarily for light transmission, making precise peak heat loss calculations critical for boiler capacity sizing and thermal curtain ROI evaluations.

2. Fundamental Thermodynamic Heat Load Transfer Equations

The steady-state nocturnal heat loss of a commercial greenhouse envelope is governed by Fourier's law of thermal conduction coupled with convective boundary layers and wind-induced air infiltration:

Q_total = Q_transmission + Q_infiltration [W]
Q_transmission = ∑ (U_i × A_i) × (T_in - T_out) [W]
Q_infiltration = 0.33 × N_ach × V_gh × (T_in - T_out) [W]

Where \(U_i\) is the overall heat transfer coefficient (W/m²·K), \(A_i\) is the exposed surface area (m²), \(T_{in} - T_{out}\) is the design temperature differential (\(\Delta T\)), \(N_{ach}\) is the air exchange rate per hour (ACH), and \(V_{gh}\) is the greenhouse volume (m³).

3. Glazing Material U-Values, Multilayer Thermal Curtains, and Infiltration Losses

  • Single Polyethylene Film (150–200 μm): \(U pprox 6.0 ext{--}6.5 ext{ W/m²·K}\). High long-wave infrared transmission; susceptible to excessive radiative condensation loss.
  • Double Poly Inflated (Air-Inflated Glazing): \(U pprox 3.2 ext{--}3.5 ext{ W/m²·K}\). Trapped 10 cm dead air barrier slashes thermal transmission by nearly 45%.
  • Single Float Glass (4 mm Horticultural Glass): \(U pprox 5.8 ext{--}6.0 ext{ W/m²·K}\). High daylight transmittance (91%) but significant nocturnal conductive dissipation.
  • Diffuse Double Glazing / Insulated Venlo: \(U pprox 2.8 ext{--}3.0 ext{ W/m²·K}\).
  • High-Reflective Aluminum Thermal Screens: Deployment of multi-layer aluminum film screens reduces composite envelope U-values by 45–65%, reflecting thermal infrared radiation back into the crop canopy and creating an insulating micro-attic airspace.
