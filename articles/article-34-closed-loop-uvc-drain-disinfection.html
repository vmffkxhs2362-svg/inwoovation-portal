Recirculating hydroponic drainage without adequate sterilization risks catastrophic systemic infection by waterborne pathogens such as *Tomato Brown Rugose Fruit Virus (ToBRFV)*, *Pythium ultimum*, and *Fusarium oxysporum*. Delivering an accurate UV-C fluence dose ($\ge 250\text{ mJ/cm}^2$ at $254\text{ nm}$) provides reliable $99.999\%$ (5-log) pathogen elimination without generating toxic chemical byproducts.
