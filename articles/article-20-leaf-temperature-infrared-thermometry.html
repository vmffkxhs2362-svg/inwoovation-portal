Leaf Temperature Infrared Thermometry & Crop Water Stress Index (CWSI) Sensing

📅 2026-08-29 🏷️ Phyto-Sensing & Thermography ✍️ Inwoovation AgTech Research

1. Why Ambient Air Temperature Is Insufficient

In high-wire greenhouse steering, crops are active thermodynamic heat exchangers. Under intense radiation (\(> 600 \text{ W/m}^2\)), active transpiration evaporatively cools the foliage, causing leaf temperature (\(T_{leaf}\)) to be 2°C to 4°C cooler than ambient air (\(T_{air}\)). Conversely, when roots experience water stress or high root zone EC, stomata close, eliminating latent heat loss and elevating \(T_{leaf}\) up to 3°C above air temperature.

2. Stefan-Boltzmann Law & Infrared Radiometry

Thermal Radiation (E, W/m²) = ε × σ × (T_leaf_Kelvin)⁴
• ε = Leaf Emissivity (0.97 - 0.98 for plant cuticle)
• σ = Stefan-Boltzmann Constant (5.67037 × 10⁻⁸ W m⁻² K⁻⁴)

Crop Water Stress Index (CWSI) = [(T_leaf − T_air) − ΔT_lower] / [ΔT_upper − ΔT_lower]

CWSI scales strictly between 0 and 1:

  • CWSI = 0.0 – 0.2 (Optimal Hydration): Maximum transpiration cooling (\(\Delta T_{lower}\) baseline); stomata fully open.
  • CWSI = 0.3 – 0.5 (Mild Generative Stress): Controlled mild deficit steering, stimulating fruit sugar accumulation without vegetative collapse.
  • CWSI > 0.6 (Critical Drought Stress): Stomatal closure (\(\Delta T_{upper}\) threshold); immediate irrigation trigger required.

Section C: Closed-Loop Control Architecture & Sensor Calibration Protocol

To eliminate actuator hunting and mechanical wear across 3-way hydronic mixing valves, rack-and-pinion roof vents, 70 bar fogging manifolds, and Venturi fertigation injectors, commercial facilities deploy a cascaded control hierarchy combining outer-loop Model Predictive Control (MPC, horizon $H_p = 24\text{ h}$, step $\Delta t = 300\text{ s}$) with inner-loop anti-windup PID controllers ($\Delta t = 1–5 ext{ s}$):

  • Hysteresis Deadbands & Slew-Rate Limiting: Roof vent and thermal curtain actuators enforce a deadband of $\pm 0.3\text{ °C}$ and $\pm 0.05\text{ kPa}$ VPD with a maximum opening velocity of $2.5\%\text{ min}^{-1}$ to prevent cold convective downdrafts onto the apical meristem.
  • Instrumentation Calibration SOP: Aspirated sensor housings (PT1000 Class A resistance thermometers, capacitive polymer RH chips, dual-beam NDIR $CO_2$ analyzers, 3-rod TDR dielectric waveguides, and double-junction KCl pH/EC probes) undergo bi-weekly two-point calibration against NIST-traceable buffer standards ($ ext{pH } 4.01 / 7.00$; $ ext{EC } 1.413 / 5.00 ext{ dS/m}$).
  • Fail-Safe Sensor Fusion & Interlocks: A real-time Kalman filter monitors innovation residuals ($|r_k| > 3\sigma$). If probe drift or open-circuit fault is detected, the edge PLC executes bumpless transfer to redundant zone sensors and enforces hardware safety limits on minimum pipe temperature and emergency vent opening.
