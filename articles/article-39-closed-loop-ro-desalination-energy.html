Closed-Loop Reverse Osmosis (RO) Desalination & Sodium ($Na^+$) Rejection Kinetics

Published: 2026-09-01 • Author: Inwoo Hwang • Reading Time: 9 min

In closed-loop hydroponics, non-essential sodium ions ($Na^+$) accumulate in recirculating drain water, eventually elevating osmotic pressure and impairing water uptake. Integrating low-energy Reverse Osmosis (RO) membranes equipped with isobaric pressure exchangers (PX) achieves $>99.2\%$ sodium rejection at specific energy consumptions under $0.85\text{ kWh/m}^3$.
