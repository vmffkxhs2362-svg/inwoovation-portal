Welcome to the central engineering monograph repository of Inwoovation Lab. Every technical article in this library formulates commercial greenhouse and vertical farm operations from first-principles physics, chemical stoichiometry, and control theory—bridging rigorous academic derivations (DIN V 18599-10, EN 13031-1, FAO-56 Penman-Monteith, Farquhar-von Caemmerer-Berry RuBisCO kinetics, and Davies/Debye-Hückel speciation) with actionable setpoints for commercial growers. You can also browse our interactive categorized filter view in the Engineering Guides & Whitepapers Portal.

1. Core Engineering Pillars & Mathematical Methodology

Across all 197 multilingual monographs, our engineering framework standardizes five critical subsystems of modern protected cropping:

  • Psychrometric & Aerodynamic Microclimate Control: Coupled leaf energy balances solving Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPDleaf in kPa), stomatal conductance (gs), Horizontal Air Flow (HAF) boundary-layer thinning, 70 bar adiabatic fogging droplet kinetics (D32 < 15 μm), and semi-closed Air Treatment Unit (ATU) enthalpy recovery on the Mollier h-x diagram.
  • Structural & Thermal Envelope Engineering: Peak heating load calculations under DIN V 18599, multi-layer aluminized thermal screen U-value reductions (6.5 → 2.1 W·m-2·K-1), hot-water buffer tank Richardson number (Ri > 10) thermocline stratification, Aquifer Thermal Energy Storage (ATES) heat pumps, and biomass/natural gas CHP selective catalytic reduction (SCR DeNOx).
  • Photobiology & Solid-State Lighting Physics: Daily Light Integral (DLI in mol·m-2·d-1), extended PAR (ePAR 400–750 nm), Emerson Enhancement Effect (660 nm Deep-Red + 730 nm Far-Red), Phytochrome Photostationary State (PSS), Beer-Lambert canopy light extinction (kdiffuse ≈ 0.52), and diode junction temperature (Tj) thermal resistance networks.
  • Closed-Loop Hydroponic Chemistry & Rhizosphere Physics: Milliequivalent (meq·L-1) cation-anion electroneutrality, Uptake Concentration Factor (UCF), Debye-Hückel/Davies ionic activity coefficients, van Genuchten substrate pF matric potential curves, TDR dielectric moisture sensing, nanobubble dissolved oxygen (DO > 25 mg·L-1), and Chick-Watson UV-C (254 nm) viral disinfection.
  • Autonomous AI Control & Agricultural Capital Economics: Multi-objective Model Predictive Control (MPC), EPEX Spot Day-Ahead negative electricity price arbitrage, 10-year CapEx/OpEx NPV & IRR financial modeling, and non-dilutive federal/state grant stacking (USDA REAP, EQIP, CDFA SWEEP, German BLE/KfW).
