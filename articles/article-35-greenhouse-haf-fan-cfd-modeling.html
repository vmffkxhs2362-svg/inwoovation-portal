Stagnant microclimates in dense high-wire crop canopies create localized cold spots and humidity spikes ($RH > 90\%$), drastically increasing fungal pathogen germination. Properly spaced Horizontal Air Flow (HAF) circulation fans generate continuous toroidal air movement ($0.3–0.5\text{ m/s}$), thinning boundary layer resistance without causing mechanical stress.
