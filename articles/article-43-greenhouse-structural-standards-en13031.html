Greenhouse Structural Standards (EN 13031-1 & NEN 3859): Wind, Snow & Crop Loads

Published: 2026-09-01 • Author: Inwoo Hwang • Reading Time: 10 min

Modern Venlo commercial greenhouses with gutter heights exceeding $6.5–8.0\text{ m}$ experience massive aerodynamic wind suction and overturning moments. Structural compliance with EN 13031-1 and NEN 3859 requires precise calculations combining dead loads, snow accumulation, dynamic wind pressure ($q_w \ge 0.50\text{ kN/m}^2$), and suspended crop trellis loads ($15–20\text{ kg/m}^2$).
