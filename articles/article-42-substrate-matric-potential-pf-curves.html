Substrate Matric Potential ($\Psi_m$), $pF$ Water Retention Curves & van Genuchten Physics

Published: 2026-09-01 • Author: Inwoo Hwang • Reading Time: 10 min

Volumetric Water Content ($VWC\%$) alone does not quantify the energy work a plant root must exert to extract water. Matric potential ($\Psi_m$, expressed as suction tension in hPa or $pF = \log_{10}|\text{hPa}|$) dictates water availability across the pore-size distribution. In rockwool slabs, Easily Available Water (EAW) resides tightly between $pF\ 1.0\text{ (10 hPa)}$ and $pF\ 2.0\text{ (100 hPa)}$.

1. The van Genuchten Soil Water Retention Curve

\Theta(h) = \left[ 1 + (\alpha |h|)^n \right]^{-m}, \quad m = 1 - 1/n

Operating beyond $pF > 2.2$ triggers sharp root hydraulic cavitation and stomatal closure.
