❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

Why does increasing greenhouse CO₂ from 420 ppm to 900–1,000 ppm boost C3 crop yield by 20–30%? ▾
Tomato, cucumber, pepper, and lettuce use C3 Rubisco carbon fixation, where atmospheric O₂ competes with CO₂ (photorespiration). Elevating intercellular CO₂ suppresses photorespiratory losses and increases light-saturated RuBP carboxylation rates.
At what roof vent opening does greenhouse CO₂ enrichment become uneconomical? ▾
Above 15–20% ridge vent aperture or > 1.5–2.0 ACH air exchange, purge leakage dominates canopy uptake. Above this threshold, climate computers drop target CO₂ from 1,000 ppm to maintenance levels (450–600 ppm).
How does Farquhar leaf biochemistry separate Rubisco-limited vs. RuBP-regeneration-limited photosynthesis? ▾
At low light or high CO₂, electron transport rate (J_max) limits RuBP regeneration; at high irradiance and lower CO₂, active Rubisco capacity (V_cmax) caps assimilation. Optimal greenhouses balance PPFD and CO₂ along this co-limitation curve.
