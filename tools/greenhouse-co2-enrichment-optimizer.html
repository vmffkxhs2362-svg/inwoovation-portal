❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How do you size greenhouse CO₂ dosing capacity (kg/ha·h) against ventilation air exchange? ▾
Multiply greenhouse volume by air-change rate (ACH) and target concentration delta (C_target - C_ambient) in kg/m³, then add net canopy photosynthesis (~35–45 kg/ha·h) to determine peak hourly CO₂ supply.
When does pure liquid tank CO₂ outperform scrubbed CHP exhaust gas for enrichment? ▾
On sunny mild spring days when thermal buffer tanks are full and firing a gas boiler/CHP purely for CO₂ wastes heat or overheats the house, pure liquid CO₂ delivers zero-heat enrichment economically.
Why does optimal CO₂ setpoint taper down above 15–20% roof vent opening? ▾
At high vent apertures, wind-driven purge losses expel > 80% of injected gas before leaves absorb it, shifting optimal economic setpoints from 1,000 ppm down toward 450–600 ppm.
