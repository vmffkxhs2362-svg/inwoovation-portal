❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How does chemical speciation differ from simple ppm nutrient dosing? ▾
Ions in concentrated solutions form uncharged ion pairs (such as CaSO₄⁰ and MgHPO₄⁰) and precipitate once ion activity products exceed mineral solubility products (K_sp). Speciation models account for activity coefficients and pH-driven phosphate shifts.
At what slab pH does brushite (CaHPO₄·2H₂O) or octacalcium phosphate precipitate on drippers? ▾
Above pH 6.3–6.5, H₂PO₄⁻ converts to HPO₄²⁻, driving rapid calcium phosphate crystal scaling inside drippers and rockwool root zones unless nitric/phosphoric acid keeps feed pH at 5.5–5.8.
Why does Davies equation accuracy matter in high-EC (3.5–5.0 mS/cm) closed recirculation loops? ▾
Multi-valent Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, and SO₄²⁻ experience steep ionic-strength activity depression; Davies adjusts thermodynamic equilibrium constants so calculated Langelier and gypsum saturation indices remain reliable.
