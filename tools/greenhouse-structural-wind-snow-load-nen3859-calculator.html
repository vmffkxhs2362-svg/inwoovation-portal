❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How do DIN EN 13031-1 and NEN 3859 calculate design wind and snow loads for Venlo greenhouses? ▾
Both codes compute peak velocity wind pressure q_p(z) from regional basic wind speed and terrain roughness, combined with multi-span valley snow drift coefficients (μ_i) and a heated-glass snow-melt thermal factor (C_t).
Why must greenhouse thermal screens be opened automatically during heavy snow warnings? ▾
Closing an insulating thermal screen traps heat inside the lower crop zone, dropping roof glass surface temperature below 0 °C (driving C_t back to 1.0) and risking dangerous snow accumulation in roof valleys.
What maximum structural deflection prevents Venlo tempered/float glass breakage? ▾
Aluminum gutters and steel lattice trusses are designed to limit vertical bending deflection under extreme serviceability load strictly within L/200 (e.g., max 20 mm over a 4.0 m gutter span) so glass panes do not crack.
