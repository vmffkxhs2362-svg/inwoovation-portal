❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

Why does Stanghellini Penman-Monteith perform better than open-field FAO-56 inside greenhouses? ▾
Open-field FAO-56 assumes 2.0 m/s turbulent wind, whereas sheltered Venlo crops experience 0.1–0.4 m/s airflow. Stanghellini explicitly adjusts leaf boundary-layer resistance (r_a) for low indoor convection and multi-layer LAI.
How quickly should greenhouse irrigation frequency respond when solar radiation doubles? ▾
Because Penman-Monteith radiation energy dominates daytime latent flux, doubling irradiance from 250 to 500 W/m² nearly doubles hourly canopy transpiration (220 to ~420 g/m²·h), requiring proportional radiation-sum drip triggering.
What is the ratio between net radiation energy (W/m²) and water transpired (L/m²·h)? ▾
Latent heat of vaporization is ~2.45 MJ/kg (680 Wh/L). Every 100 W/m² converted cleanly into transpiration evaporates roughly 147 g/m²·h (0.147 L/m²·h) of water.
