❓ Frequently Asked Engineering & Ag-Finance Questions (FAQ)

How does Ertragswert (capitalized earnings value) differ from Substanzwert (asset replacement value) in German Hofnachfolge? ▾
In German agricultural appraisal (Landwirtschaftliche Taxationslehre), Substanzwert measures the physical replacement cost of land, glasshouses, and boilers, which often exceeds €3M–€6M. However, commercial farm succession (Hofnachfolge) and Landwirtschaftliche Rentenbank financing evaluate the operation primarily on Ertragswert—the capitalized sustainable net operating cash flow (EBITDA minus operator remuneration and reinvestment). Structuring a succession via Ertragspacht or phased equity tranches anchored to Ertragswert prevents over-leveraging the incoming Meister operator.
What technical measures unlock the maximum 40% BLE federal grant rate in Germany? ▾
Individual component replacements (such as installing thermal screens or LED fixtures) typically qualify for base grant tiers, whereas holistic system modernizations verified by an accredited energy auditor—combining multi-layer thermal/blackout screens, hydronic buffer tanks, heat-pump integration, and climate computer predictive control achieving ≥25% primary CO₂ reduction—unlock the highest BLE subsidy tiers (up to 35%–40% of eligible investment costs).
How does the BEHG carbon price affect greenhouse heating ROI between 2026 and 2030? ▾
Natural gas combustion emits approximately 0.201 kg CO₂ per kWh of thermal energy. As carbon pricing transitions from national fixed corridors into the European ETS-2 market (€65 to €120+ per metric ton of CO₂), every 1,000 MWh of natural gas saved avoids 201 tons of CO₂ liability, accelerating the payback period of double thermal screens and heat pumps by 1.2 to 2.0 years.
