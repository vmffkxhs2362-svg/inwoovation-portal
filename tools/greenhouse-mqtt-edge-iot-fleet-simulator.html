❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

Why do greenhouse IoT deployments prefer LoRaWAN and RS-485 over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi? ▾
Water-filled dense crop foliage and steel truss spans strongly scatter and absorb 2.4 GHz signals. Sub-GHz 868/915 MHz LoRaWAN penetrates multi-hectare foliage cleanly, while shielded RS-485 provides deterministic wired control.
What is the practical difference between MQTT QoS 0 and QoS 1 for fertigation and climate alarms? ▾
QoS 0 is fire-and-forget suitable for high-frequency diagnostics, whereas QoS 1 enforces PUBACK confirmation so critical low-tank-level, boiler-fault, or frost alarms never vanish silently over spotty farm WAN links.
How do edge gateways prevent data loss during internet outages in rural greenhouse clusters? ▾
An industrial edge gateway stores incoming JSON sensor payloads in a local SQLite or flash ring buffer and replays chronologically to the cloud broker once cellular or fiber backhaul reconnects.
