❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How do you convert canopy PPFD (µmol/m²/s) into Daily Light Integral (DLI in mol/m²/d)? ▾
Multiply instantaneous PPFD (µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹) by photoperiod hours and by 0.0036 (3,600 seconds/hour divided by 1,000,000 µmol/mol). For example, 250 µmol/m²/s over 16 hours equals exactly 14.4 mol/m²/d.
How does 730 nm Far-Red light drive the Emerson Enhancement Effect and leaf expansion? ▾
Combining 730 nm Far-Red with 660 nm deep-red co-excites Photosystems I and II simultaneously (+10–15% quantum yield) while phytochrome shade-avoidance signaling expands leaf capture area and accelerates tomato truss elongation.
What blue photon fraction (400–500 nm) prevents hypocotyl stretching in leafy greens and seedlings? ▾
Maintaining 12–20% blue photons suppresses cryptochrome-mediated stem elongation, thickens leaf cuticles, and boosts anthocyanin/chlorophyll density without incurring excessive red-photon efficiency loss.
