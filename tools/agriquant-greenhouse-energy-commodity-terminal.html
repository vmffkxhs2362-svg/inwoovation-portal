❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How is the greenhouse CHP Clean Spark Spread calculated accurately? ▾
Clean Spark Spread subtracts fuel input cost (gas price divided by electrical efficiency) and carbon allowance costs from spot electricity revenue, then adds the avoided boiler fuel value of captured CHP thermal water and scrubbed CO₂ exhaust.
At what electricity-to-gas price ratio is a greenhouse heat pump cheaper than a condensing gas boiler? ▾
A heat pump beats a 95%-efficient condensing gas boiler whenever its seasonal COP exceeds (Electricity Price / Gas Price) × 0.95. At €0.14/kWh power and €0.06/kWh gas, the break-even COP is roughly 2.2.
What does 95% Energy Margin Value-at-Risk (VaR) tell greenhouse operators? ▾
95% daily or seasonal VaR estimates the maximum adverse operational energy margin swing expected 19 days out of 20 under historical gas and power volatility, guiding fixed forward hedging versus spot exposure.
