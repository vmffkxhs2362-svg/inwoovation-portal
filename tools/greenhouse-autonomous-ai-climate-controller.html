❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

What is the primary advantage of Model Predictive Control (MPC) over standard greenhouse PID computers? ▾
PID loops react only after temperature or humidity errors occur, creating costly boiler-vent overshoot cycles. MPC solves a 24-to-48-hour forecast trajectory every 5 minutes, pre-closing screens and coasting solar inertia automatically.
How does tuning the Yield vs. Energy optimization weight change actuator trajectories? ▾
Increasing Yield weight prioritizes stable 24-hour photosynthesis targets (maintaining daytime CO₂ and optimal leaf VPD), while shifting toward Energy Savings extends thermal screen retention and accepts wider temperature bands within crop tolerance.
What safety guardrails prevent AI controllers from damaging crops during sensor drift? ▾
Production autonomous controllers enforce hard physiological bounds (T_min/T_max, VPD_max, dew-point condensation lockouts) and revert deterministically to fallback rule-based PID loops whenever sensor covariance anomalies occur.
