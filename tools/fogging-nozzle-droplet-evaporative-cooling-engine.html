❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

Why do commercial greenhouses require 70-bar high-pressure fogging instead of 5-bar misting? ▾
High-pressure 70-bar nozzles atomize water into 5–15 μm micro-droplets that flash-evaporate entirely in mid-air before reaching the canopy, whereas coarse 5-bar droplets wet leaves and trigger Botrytis and fungal disease.
How much cooling (ΔT °C) can adiabatic fogging deliver in humid vs. arid climates? ▾
Maximum evaporative cooling equals the wet-bulb depression (T_dry - T_wet): up to 10–14 °C cooling in arid desert CEA (15% RH), versus 2–4 °C in humid summer climates where air approaches saturation quickly.
Why must high-pressure fogging water be Reverse Osmosis (RO) or softened demineralized water? ▾
Dissolved calcium carbonate and silica precipitate instantaneously on 0.15 mm stainless nozzle orifices as droplets flash off, plugging nozzles and coating glass and foliage in white mineral scale.
