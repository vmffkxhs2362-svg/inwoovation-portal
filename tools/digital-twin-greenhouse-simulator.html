❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How does a physics-based greenhouse digital twin predict indoor climate trajectories? ▾
It solves coupled hourly differential equations for solar shortwave transmission, glazing thermal conduction (Q = U·A·ΔT), roof vent mass exchange, canopy Penman-Monteith transpiration, and pipe-rail heat release simultaneously.
What happens thermodynamically when an aluminized thermal screen closes by 98% instead of 100%? ▾
Leaving a deliberate 2–5% humidity crack lets cold upper glass condense moisture while retaining ~80% of conductive heat savings, preventing Botrytis condensation spikes on the crop.
Why does PID overshoot waste boiler fuel compared to anticipatory predictive steering? ▾
Reactive PID controllers hold hydronic pipes hot even 30 minutes before sunrise solar gain arrives, overheating the compartment and forcing ridge vents to purge paid thermal energy.
