⚖️ California Grant Stacking & Anti-Double-Dipping Guidelines
• Federal & State Stacking Permitted: California state grants (e.g. CDFA SWEEP) may be combined with federal programs (e.g. USDA REAP), provided that cumulative grant funding does not exceed 100% of eligible equipment costs.
• No Double-Dipping on Identical Invoices: Submitting the identical capital invoice or equipment serial number for 100% reimbursement across multiple programs (e.g. CARB FARMER and NRCS EQIP) is strictly prohibited. Costs must be prorated across verified cost-share schedules.
• Disadvantaged Community (DAC) Bonus: Farms located within SB 535 Disadvantaged Communities receive 10–15 priority points during state evaluation, significantly increasing funding award probability.
