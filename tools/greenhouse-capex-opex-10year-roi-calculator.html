❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

Why is 10-year Levered Equity IRR higher than Unlevered Project IRR when bank debt costs 5%? ▾
Positive financial leverage amplifies equity returns whenever unlevered asset yield (e.g., 11–13% EBITDA/CapEx) exceeds the after-tax interest rate on long-term agricultural infrastructure loans, provided DSCR ≥ 1.25×.
Which operating cost parameter causes the largest swing in 10-year greenhouse NPV? ▾
In temperate and cold climates, thermal energy price (€/kWh gas or grid tariff) alongside annual marketable fruit yield (kg/m²) dominates NPV sensitivity; installing double screens and heat pumps cuts energy variance sharply.
How do government CapEx subsidies alter 10-year break-even payback timelines? ▾
A 35–40% non-dilutive grant reduces upfront equity investment without lowering future crop revenues, typically compressing simple payback from 7+ years down to 3.2–4.5 years.
