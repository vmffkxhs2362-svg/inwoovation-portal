❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

What horizontal air velocity (m/s) optimizes greenhouse transpiration without inducing stomatal closure? ▾
Maintaining uniform 0.3 to 0.6 m/s air speed shrinks leaf boundary-layer thickness, preventing humid stagnant pockets and improving CO₂ mass transfer; speeds above 1.2 m/s cause wind-chill desiccation and mechanical stress.
How should Horizontal Airflow (HAF) fans be arranged for maximum circulation efficiency? ▾
Space fans in a racetrack opposite-row loop roughly every 12–15 meters at 1.5× gutter spacing, tilting slightly downward 5° to sweep beneath overhead trusses while delivering ~0.03–0.05 m³/s per m² floor area.
How does air velocity directly prevent calcium tipburn in vertical farm lettuce? ▾
Zero-wind enclosed rooms leave 99% RH boundary layers around tight inner meristems, halting daytime transpiration. Direct vertical laminar jets (0.4–0.6 m/s) restore xylem calcium delivery to expanding heart leaves.
