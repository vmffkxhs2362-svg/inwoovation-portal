❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

Why does percent UV transmittance (% UV-T) govern hydroponic drain disinfection sizing? ▾
According to the Beer-Lambert law, dissolved organics, tannins, and Fe-EDDHA chelates absorb 254 nm UV-C photons exponentially. Dropping from 75% to 35% UV-T cuts effective quartz sleeve dose sharply unless flow rate slows or pre-filtration clarifies runoff.
What UV-C fluence dose (mJ/cm²) neutralizes Pythium, Fusarium, and viruses in drain water? ▾
Zoospore oomycetes like Pythium require ~60–100 mJ/cm², fungal Fusarium oxysporum spores need 150–250 mJ/cm², and resilient tobamoviruses (ToBRFV / TMV) demand 250–400+ mJ/cm² combined with 5 μm mechanical pre-filtration.
Does UV-C irradiation degrade chelated iron (Fe-EDDHA / Fe-DTPA) in recirculated fertigation? ▾
Yes. High 254 nm doses photolyze phenolic Fe-chelate ligands into precipitated iron oxide sludge, reducing % UV-T further; compensate by dosing fresh Fe chelate downstream after UV disinfection.
