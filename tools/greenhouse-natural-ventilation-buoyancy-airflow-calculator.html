📐 Thermodynamic Model

This calculator uses the combined buoyancy + wind-driven natural ventilation model widely used in greenhouse engineering research (Kittas et al., 1997; Boulard & Baille, 1995):

Buoyancy-driven (Stack Effect):

Q_buoyancy = Cd × A_vent × √(g × ΔH × (T_in − T_out) / T_mean_K)

Where g = 9.81 m/s², ΔH = height difference between inlet and outlet vents, and T_mean_K = mean absolute temperature (K).

Wind-driven:

Q_wind = Cd × A_vent × U_wind × √(Cw)

Combined (quadrature sum):

Q_total = √(Q_buoyancy² + Q_wind²)

The combined model assumes buoyancy and wind effects are independent and additive in quadrature (ASABE EP406.4, ISO 13790:2008).
