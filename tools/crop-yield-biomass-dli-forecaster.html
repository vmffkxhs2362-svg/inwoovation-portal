❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

What is Radiation Use Efficiency (RUE / LUE) in commercial fruiting greenhouse crops? ▾
Light Use Efficiency measures grams of fresh fruit produced per mole of intercepted PAR photons: high-wire vine tomatoes typically yield 2.6–3.4 g fresh weight per mol PAR (equating to ~0.75–1.0% yield boost per +1% light).
How does source-sink balance cap yield gains when DLI increases above 30 mol/m²·d? ▾
Above 30–35 mol/m²·d, sink strength (truss pruning fruit load and cultivar fruit size) and CO₂ availability constrain harvest conversion. Without adding stem density or fruit count, excess photons dissipate as heat or photoinhibition.
Why do growers model winter vs. summer harvest curves using 3-week fruit lag? ▾
Assimilates partitioned into flowering trusses today appear as ripe harvested tomato or pepper biomass 42 to 56 days later depending on 24-hour temperature sum, making weekly DLI tracking vital for contract supply forecasting.
