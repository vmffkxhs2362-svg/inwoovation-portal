🔬 Engineering Theory & Biophysical Principles

Closed-loop recirculating greenhouse horticulture mandates 100% recycling of drain runoff to comply with environmental nitrogen/phosphorus discharge mandates. However, recirculation presents severe biosecurity vulnerabilities: zoosporic oomycetes such as Pythium ultimum and Phytophthora cryptogea, as well as Fusarium oxysporum and viral pathogens, spread rapidly throughout the shared irrigation loop. Physical disinfection via ultraviolet germicidal irradiation (UV-C at 254 nm) disrupts microbial DNA/RNA pyrimidine dimers, inactivating pathogens without depositing phytotoxic chemical residues. Meanwhile, reverse osmosis (RO) membrane arrays strip ballast sodium ions (Na⁺) to maintain low electrical conductivity.

UV-C Germicidal Dose (mJ/cm²) = Fluence Rate (mW/cm²) × Exposure Residence Time (seconds) • Inactivation Equation: N / N_0 = exp(-k × Dose) • Pythium / Phytophthora Inactivation Target: ≥ 100 mJ/cm² (99.9% 3-log kill) • Fusarium Target: ≥ 250 mJ/cm² (due to melanized thick-walled conidia)

❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

Why does UV transmission (% UV-T) dictate the disinfection performance of drainage water? ▾
Drainage runoff contains dissolved humic acids, organic root exudates, and suspended iron chelates that absorb 254nm photons. If UV-T drops from 90% to 60%, the effective germicidal radiation penetrating the fluid decreases exponentially, requiring slower flow rates or pre-filtration to maintain kill dosage.
How does reverse osmosis membrane flux decay over time in greenhouse service? ▾
Dissolved calcium sulfate (gypsum) and silica precipitate on the polyamide membrane surface, while biofilm creates a biological foul layer. Monitoring the Specific Energy Consumption (kWh/m³ permeate) and Normalized Transmembrane Pressure (TMP) enables precise scheduling of chemical Clean-In-Place (CIP) cycles.
Can UV-C treatment destroy chelated micronutrients in recirculating fertilizer water? ▾
Yes. High-dose UV-C radiation photochemically degrades synthetic chelating agents like Fe-EDTA and Fe-DTPA into free ferric iron, which then precipitates as insoluble rust. Commercial facilities utilize photostable iron complexes such as Fe-EDDHA or inject chelated iron downstream of the UV reactor.

❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (Technical FAQ)

Why must calcium nitrate and sulfate/phosphate fertilizers be separated into A and B stock tanks?

Mixing concentrated calcium ions (Ca²⁺) with sulfate (SO₄²⁻) or orthophosphate (H₂PO₄⁻) in the same stock tank causes immediate precipitation of insoluble gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) and calcium phosphate once the solubility product (Ksp) is exceeded.

How do you calculate the required acid volume for hydroponic bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻) alkalinity neutralization?

Subtract target residual buffer alkalinity (typically 0.5 to 0.8 mmol/L HCO₃⁻ for pH 5.6–5.8 stability) from raw water bicarbonate concentration, then stoichiometry-match with nitric acid (HNO₃) or phosphoric acid (H₃PO₄) normality.

What drain percentage and slab EC dryback should be targeted in rockwool and cocopeat substrates?

During high-radiation summer days, target 25% to 35% daily drainage fraction with an overnight volumetric water content (VWC) dryback of 8% to 12% for vegetative steering, or 12% to 18% dryback for generative steering.
