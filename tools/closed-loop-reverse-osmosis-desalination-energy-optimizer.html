❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How does Van 't Hoff osmotic pressure dictate Reverse Osmosis feed pressure? ▾
Water naturally flows toward higher salinity; RO feed pumps must supply osmotic pressure π (roughly 0.08 bar per 100 ppm TDS) plus membrane hydraulic drop and target Net Driving Pressure (NDP) to sustain permeate flux.
Why do greenhouse RO plants acidify feed water or dose antiscalant before membranes? ▾
Concentrating brine to 75–80% recovery raises Calcium and Bicarbonate saturation inside the last RO elements. Acidification (pH 5.8–6.2) or antiscalant dosing keeps the Langelier Saturation Index (LSI) negative to prevent irreversible CaCO₃ scale.
Must pure RO permeate water be remineralized before mixing hydroponic nutrients? ▾
Yes. Ultra-pure RO permeate (< 0.05 mS/cm) lacks bicarbonate alkalinity (HCO₃⁻) and calcium, making pH unstable in rockwool slabs unless buffered to 0.5–0.8 mmol/L HCO₃⁻.
