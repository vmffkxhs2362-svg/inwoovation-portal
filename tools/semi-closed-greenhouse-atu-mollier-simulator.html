🏛️ Semi-Closed Greenhouse Thermodynamics & ATU Engineering Principles

Commercial semi-closed glasshouses—pioneered by Dutch benchmarks such as Kubo Ultra-Clima and Van der Hoeven ModulAIR—overcome the structural dilemma of passive ridge ventilation. Traditional greenhouses must open roof vents widely during warm summer afternoons, flushing enriched CO₂ into the atmosphere and exposing crops to migrating thrips and whiteflies.

1. Under-Gutter Perforated PE Duct Airflow & Boundary-Layer Decoupling

Conditioned air from the pressurized Air Treatment Unit (ATU) corridor is distributed evenly beneath every hanging crop gutter through 600–900 mm clear polyethylene convection tubes. Controlled turbulent air jets (4–8 m/s) exiting calibrated side orifices break stagnant laminar leaf boundary layers, homogenizing horizontal temperatures within ±0.8 °C and accelerating stomatal CO₂ uptake and calcium transpiration flux.

2. Governing Mollier h-x Psychrometric Equations

• Moist Air Specific Enthalpy (h): h = c_pa · T + x · (r_0 + c_pv · T) = 1.006 · T + x · (2501 + 1.86 · T) [kJ/kg dry air] • High-Pressure Adiabatic Fog Cooling (Isenthalpic Humidification): h_4 ≈ h_3 (Sensible dry-bulb drop toward wet-bulb temperature as latent moisture ratio x rises) • Adiabatic Mixing Chamber Energy & Mass Balance: T_mix = α · T_out + (1 - α) · T_return x_mix = α · x_out + (1 - α) · x_return • Canopy Overpressure & Pest Exclusion Gauge Pressure: ΔP = 0.5 · ρ · (v_fan² - v_leak²) ≈ 15 ~ 30 Pa

3. Winter Latent Heat Recovery & Carbon Retention

During cold nights and overcast winter mornings, 80–90% of warm, humid greenhouse air is recirculated through the ATU cooling/dehumidification coils to recover canopy transpiration latent heat via heat-pump heat exchangers. Compared with standard Venlo vents, semi-closed ATUs cut seasonal boiler heating energy by 25% to 35% while stabilizing 900–1,000 ppm CO₂ enrichment continuously.

4. Technical Engineering FAQs

Q1. What is the optimal positive overpressure setpoint for commercial insect exclusion?

Commercial glasshouses typically maintain +15 Pa to +25 Pa over ambient outdoor pressure under 4–6 m/s wind loads. Above +35 Pa, structural uplift forces and glass glazing gasket stress rise sharply, so proportional roof exhaust louvers modulate automatically to cap pressure.

Q2. Why do 100-bar ATU fogging chambers outperform conventional pad-and-fan cooling walls?

Pad-and-fan systems suffer 4–8 °C thermal gradients between air intake and exhaust fans. Complete 10–15 μm droplet flash evaporation inside the ATU plenum followed by under-gutter perforated PE distribution keeps canopy horizontal variance below ±0.8 °C.

Q3. How are under-gutter perforated PE ducts sized to maintain uniform airflow over 80 m rows?

Designers place one 750 mm duct per 8.0–9.6 m Venlo span (or dual ducts for dense tomatoes) and follow Static Regain orifice spacing—gradually reducing hole pitch or slightly enlarging orifice area toward the capped distal end to compensate for frictional pressure loss.

❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (Technical FAQ)

How do you calculate greenhouse Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) accurately using leaf temperature?

True canopy VPD must be calculated from saturated vapor pressure at actual radiometric leaf temperature (es(T_leaf) via the Tetens equation) minus ambient air vapor pressure (ea(T_air, RH)). Assuming T_leaf = T_air introduces a 0.25 to 0.45 kPa error under high solar radiation.

What is the optimal VPD target range for commercial greenhouse crops?

Vegetative propagation requires 0.4 to 0.7 kPa, active vegetative growth thrives at 0.8 to 1.1 kPa, and generative fruit set in tomato, bell pepper, and strawberry performs best between 1.1 and 1.4 kPa to balance stomatal CO2 uptake with xylem calcium mass flow.

How do you size a greenhouse dehumidifier or Air Treatment Unit (ATU) for night-time latent moisture removal?

Multiply canopy night-time transpiration rate (typically 15 to 35 g/m²·h) by cultivated floor area (m²), then divide by the specific moisture extraction rate (L/kWh) at the target dew-point temperature on the Mollier h-x psychrometric chart.
