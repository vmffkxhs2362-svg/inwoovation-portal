❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions (FAQ)

How do slab dryback (% ΔVWC) and irrigation start/stop timing steer crops generatively vs. vegetatively? ▾
Large overnight drybacks (12–18% ΔVWC), delayed morning first shot (2–3h post-sunrise), and elevated dripper EC induce mild osmotic stress that shifts assimilates toward flowers and fruit (generative). Small drybacks (6–9%) favor leaf expansion (vegetative).
How do 24-hour mean temperature and Day-Night DIF interact with DLI during crop steering? ▾
24-hour temperature must scale proportionally with daily light integral (P24 / DLI ratio ≈ 0.8–1.0 °C per mol): high night temperatures without matching daytime photosynthesis exhaust carbohydrate reserves, causing thin stems and weak trusses.
What VPD and EC range triggers tipburn or blossom-end rot during aggressive generative steering? ▾
Combining excessive slab EC (> 4.5 mS/cm) with persistently high or near-zero VPD stalls xylem calcium transport to expanding fruit tips and meristems. Keep mid-day VPD between 0.9 and 1.3 kPa while adjusting EC incrementally.
