1. Structural, Thermal & Psychrometric Modeling Standards

Glasshouse geometry, truss steel sizing, and snow/wind actions in VenloCAD 3D and regional climate hubs reference DIN EN 13031-1 and Dutch NEN 3859 horticultural structural codes.
