## 🎯 Scoring Optimization Cheat-Sheet (USDA REAP 100-Point System)

To guarantee funding in competitive national pooling rounds, your application must score **85+ points**:

1. **Environmental Benefits (10 Points)**:
   - Prove net greenhouse gas (GHG) reduction in metric tons $CO_2e$/year using EPA AVERT and eGRID factors.
   - Include renewable thermal energy generation (ATES heat pump, biomass CHP, solar thermal).
2. **Energy Generated / Replaced (15 Points)**:
   - Energy efficiency projects replacing $\ge 35\%$ of historical electricity or natural gas consumption automatically receive maximum tier points.
3. **Commitment of Funds (15 Points)**:
   - Maintain third-party bank pre-qualification letter for the matching non-grant portion (typically 50% or 75% match).
4. **Previous Grantees (15 Points)**:
   - First-time USDA REAP applicants receive full 15 points.
5. **Simple Payback Period (15 Points)**:
   - Subsidized payback period under 4.0 years receives 15 points; under 6.0 years receives 10 points. Use our Cash Flow Sheet to demonstrate payback under 3.5 years.
6. **Underserved / Disaster Areas (10 Points)**:
   - Projects located in USDA Disadvantaged Community (DAC) or Energy Community zones receive 10 bonus points.

## ⚡ Step-by-Step Submission Instructions

1. **SAM.gov & UEI Registration**: Ensure your farm business entity holds an active Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) on [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov). (Takes 10–14 business days).
2. **Form RD 4280-3A / 3B**: Open your state's specific intake portal and copy the sections from `01_EXECUTIVE_FEASIBILITY_NARRATIVE_TEMPLATE.md` directly into the designated narrative boxes.
3. **Attach Energy Audit**: Convert `02_VDI4640_ASABE_ENERGY_AUDIT_WORKSHEET.md` with your facility's customized utility bill data to PDF as Appendix A.
4. **Vendor Quote Verification**: Attach certified equipment manufacturer quotes (from our B2B RFQ network or local contractors) as Appendix B.
5. **Submit 30 Days Prior to State Window**: Submit by March 31 or September 30 quarterly cycle cutoff.
