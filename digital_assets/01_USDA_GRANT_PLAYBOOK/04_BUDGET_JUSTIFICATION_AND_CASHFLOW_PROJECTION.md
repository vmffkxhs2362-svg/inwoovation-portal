## 3. DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE RATIO (DSCR) SENSITIVITY

Agricultural banks mandate a minimum DSCR of $1.25\times$.

$$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{Net Operational Savings Available for Debt Service}}{\text{Annual Debt Service}} = \frac{\$84,200 - \$3,500}{\$40,832} = \mathbf{1.98\times}$$

* **Base Case**: **$1.98\times$** (Substantially exceeds bank covenant threshold).
* **Stress Test A (15% Lower Energy Savings)**: $\$68,595 / \$40,832 = \mathbf{1.68\times}$ (Safe).
* **Stress Test B (10% Higher Interest Rate)**: $\mathbf{1.81\times}$ (Safe).
