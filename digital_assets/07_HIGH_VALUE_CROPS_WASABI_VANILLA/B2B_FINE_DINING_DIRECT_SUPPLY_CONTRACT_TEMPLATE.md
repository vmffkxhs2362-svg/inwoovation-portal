### RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Purchaser operates high-end culinary establishments and desires to secure a reliable, exclusive, and continuous supply of fresh harvest-to-door Sawa-Wasabi rhizomes and cured Grade A Bourbon vanilla beans;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and commercial terms herein contained, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### ARTICLE 1: SCOPE OF SUPPLY & PRODUCTS

The Supplier agrees to sell and deliver, and the Purchaser agrees to purchase and receive, the following specialty botanical crops according to the terms of this Agreement:

1. **Product Category A**: Fresh Hydroponic Sawa-Wasabi Rhizomes (*Eutrema japonicum*, Grade 1 Gourmet).
2. **Product Category B**: Naturally Fermented Bourbon Vanilla Beans (*Vanilla planifolia*, Grade A Black Gourmet).
3. **Product Category C**: Fresh Culinary Wasabi Petioles and Foliage (*Wasabi Greens / Fine Dining Herb*).

---

### ARTICLE 2: QUALITY SPECIFICATIONS & GRADING STANDARDS

All agricultural goods delivered under this Agreement shall satisfy the following sensory, physical, and biochemical specifications:

#### 2.1. Fresh Sawa-Wasabi (Grade 1 Gourmet)
- **Unit Weight**: Minimum **120 grams** net weight per individual rhizome (trimmed with a 2.0 cm petiole collar).
- **Physical Texture**: Firm cellular turgidity, vibrant emerald green coloration, and zero percent (0%) internal core darkening or hollow heart necrosis.
- **Biochemical Profile**: High concentration of sinigrin and allyl isothiocyanate (> 2.8 mg/g FW), delivering bright, sweet initial pungency followed by aromatic herbal sweetness upon grinding on sharkskin *oroshigane*.

#### 2.2. Bourbon Vanilla Beans (Grade A Black Gourmet)
- **Pod Dimensions**: Minimum total length of **16.0 cm** per cured pod.
- **Moisture Content**: Maintained between **30.0% and 33.0%** water content.
- **Texture & Appearance**: Plump, flexible, oily sheen with leather-like suppleness that curls around fingers without splitting or cracking.
- **Vanillin Potency**: Minimum natural vanillin content of **2.0%** on a dry weight basis, free from artificial additives, synthetic ethyl vanillin, or coumarin.

### ARTICLE 3: ORDERING PROTOCOL, MOQ & COLD-CHAIN SLA

1. **Weekly Standing Order Cutoff**: The Purchaser shall transmit weekly volume forecasts to the Supplier in writing (electronic mail or authorized EDI portal) by **Monday 14:00 local time** for mid-week dispatch, and **Wednesday 14:00 local time** for weekend service dispatch.
2. **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)**:
   - Fresh Sawa-Wasabi Rhizomes: Minimum **1.0 kg** per weekly shipment.
   - Cured Bourbon Vanilla Pods: Minimum **200 grams** per monthly shipment.
   - Wasabi Culinary Petioles: Minimum **1.0 kg** per order.
3. **Harvest-to-Door Cold-Chain Logistics SLA**:
   - The Supplier warrants that all fresh wasabi rhizomes and petioles are harvested, sanitized in chilled ozone micro-bubble wash, vacuum-sealed in breathable botanical film, and packed with calibrated thermal refrigerant gel packs within **24 hours of scheduled dispatch**.
   - Transportation shall be executed via express refrigerated transit maintaining continuous core air temperatures of **$2.0^\circ\text{C}$ to $6.0^\circ\text{C}$** directly to the Purchaser's kitchen receiving dock.

### ARTICLE 4: CONTRACT PRICING & PAYMENT TERMS

1. **Contract Firm Prices** (Exclusive of applicable local sales taxes / statutory zero-rated agricultural produce):
   - **Fresh Sawa-Wasabi Grade 1**: **$165.00 USD / kg** (Fixed 12-month guaranteed contract price, insulated from open-market spot volatility).
   - **Fresh Wasabi Petioles / Foliage**: **$20.00 USD / kg**.
   - **Bourbon Vanilla Beans Grade A**: **$550.00 USD / kg** ($55.00 USD per 100g vacuum pack).
2. **Payment Terms (Net 30 Days)**:
   - The Supplier shall issue a detailed commercial invoice at the close of each calendar month covering all accepted shipments during said billing cycle.
   - The Purchaser shall remit full payment within **thirty (30) calendar days** from the invoice date via wire transfer to the Supplier's designated corporate bank account.

---

### ARTICLE 5: INSPECTION, REJECTION & REPLACEMENT REMEDIES

1. **Receiving Inspection**: The Purchaser's receiving chef or kitchen manager shall inspect shipments upon delivery for weight verification, packaging seal integrity, core temperature, and physical condition.
2. **Notice of Non-Conformity**: In the unlikely event of transit spoilage, freeze damage, transit bruising, or failure to meet Article 2 quality grades, the Purchaser shall notify the Supplier within **twenty-four (24) hours** of receipt, accompanied by photographic or video evidence.
3. **Remedy**: Upon verification of legitimate defects, the Supplier shall, at the Purchaser's election, either (i) dispatch an emergency priority replacement shipment within 24 hours at the Supplier's sole cost, or (ii) credit the invoice amount against the subsequent monthly statement.

---

### ARTICLE 6: EXCLUSIVITY & FARM-TO-TABLE CO-BRANDING

1. **Menu Attribution Rights**: The Purchaser is granted the exclusive right within its geographic municipality to feature the Supplier's certified farm brand on dining room menus, tasting course notes, digital marketing, and sommelier briefings (e.g., *"Single-Origin Sawa-Wasabi hand-harvested by [Supplier Name] CEA High-Tech Farm"*).
2. **Collaborative Promotion**: The Supplier may list the Purchaser as an official culinary partner and fine-dining showcase account in its corporate investor dossiers and media releases.

---

### ARTICLE 7: TERM, RENEWAL & TERMINATION

1. **Term**: This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect for an initial period of **one (1) year** commencing on the Effective Date.
2. **Automatic Renewal**: This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least **thirty (30) days** prior to the expiration of the then-current term.
3. **Termination for Cause**: Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect upon seven (7) days written notice if the other Party commits a material breach (such as repeated non-payment exceeding 45 days or consecutive delivery failures of grade-compliant produce) and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.
