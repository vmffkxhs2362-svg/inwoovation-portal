## 1. Executive Summary & Market Paradigm

Commercial greenhouse agriculture is caught in an escalating commodity trap:
- Commodity lettuce and tomatoes sell for **$1.50 to $3.50 per kilogram** (₩2,000 to ₩5,000/kg).
- Rising energy, fertilizer, and labor costs continually compress grower margins.

In contrast, ultra-premium specialty crops decouple greenhouse revenue from commodity wholesale auction volatility:
1. **Fresh Sawa-Wasabi Rhizomes (*Wasabia japonica*)**:
   - Market Wholesale Price: **$120 to $180 per kilogram** (₩160,000 to ₩250,000 / kg).
   - Market Demand: High-end Omakase sushi establishments, luxury hotel dining rooms, and craft distilleries facing severe authentic Japanese supply shortages.
2. **Cured Gourmet Bourbon Vanilla Beans (*Vanilla planifolia*)**:
   - Market Wholesale Price: **$350 to $600 per kilogram** (₩450,000 to ₩800,000 / kg).
   - Market Demand: Artisanal chocolatiers, Michelin-starred pastry chefs, and boutique perfumers seeking single-origin, traceable European/Korean greenhouse beans.

This suite provides the precise **biological recipes, environmental setpoints, post-harvest curing chemistry, and B2B commercial sales contracts** required to produce and monetize these ultra-high-margin crops.
