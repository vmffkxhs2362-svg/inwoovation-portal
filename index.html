❓ Core CEA Engineering Reference, Methodology & Legal Disclaimer (Click to Expand) ASABE · DIN V 18599 · FAO-56 ▾

1. Leaf-to-Air VPD vs. Relative Humidity: Relative Humidity ignores leaf boundary-layer temperature. Leaf-to-Air VPD quantifies the true vapor pressure gradient in kPa governing stomatal conductance and mass-flow Calcium xylem transport.

2. German Gärtnermeister 24h Radiation-Temperature Integration (RTI): Modulates day/night temperatures proportionally to daily solar radiation sum (J/cm²) with a pre-night drop (Vornacht-DIP) to optimize assimilate partitioning and save 15–25% heating energy.
