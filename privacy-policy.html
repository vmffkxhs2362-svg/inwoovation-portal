Privacy Policy

Last Modified: September 09, 2026

Inwoovation Engineering Lab ("Inwoovation", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website inwoovation.com. We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring transparent handling of data in compliance with international privacy standards, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Scope and Nature of Services

Inwoovation is an open-access scientific engineering portal and computational simulation platform. Users can browse technical articles, run browser-based CEA microclimate calculators, and access simulation tools without registration or providing personal identifiable information (PII).

2. Information Automatically Collected

When you visit or interact with our site, standard non-identifying technical metadata may be logged automatically by our servers or analytics providers:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (anonymized/truncated where required by GDPR)
  • Browser type, operating system, and hardware architecture
  • Pages visited, referring URL, timestamp, and session duration
  • Client-side calculation inputs (processed 100% locally in your browser and never transmitted to our backend)

3. Use of Cookies and Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use standard HTTP cookies and local storage to optimize user experience, remember interface preferences, and serve contextually relevant advertisements:

  • Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies: Google and its certified advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites on the internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative.
  • Browser Cookie Controls: You can configure your browser to block, refuse, or alert you about cookies at any time. Note that blocking cookies does not disable any computational simulation engines.

4. Data Controller and GDPR Compliance

Under the GDPR, Inwoovation acts as the Data Controller for technical log data. Our legal basis for processing non-identifying server logs is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) to maintain site security, prevent DDoS attacks, and troubleshoot server performance.

5. Open Source Philosophy & Zero-Payment Policy

Inwoovation is committed to 100% free and open-access precision agriculture biophysics. All 35 computational simulators, engineering models, and educational suites are distributed freely under open-source standards. We do not collect credit cards, process commercial transactions, or maintain paid paywalls on this platform. Source code and models are openly hosted on GitHub.

7. Google AdSense DoubleClick DART Cookie & IAB TCF v2.2 Disclosures

To sustain free public access to our 37+ horticultural engineering simulators, 3D WebGL Venlo digital twins, and agro-climatology databases without charging subscription fees, Inwoovation Engineering Lab displays contextual and personalized advertisements via Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-8597809158257497). In accordance with Google Publisher Policies and the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2):

  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie and related advertising identifiers) to serve ads on inwoovation.com based on a user's prior visits to our portal and other websites across the internet.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie and personalized advertising at any time by visiting the Google My Ad Center (Ads Settings). Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies globally via the Digital Advertising Alliance consumer choice portal at www.aboutads.info/choices or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance portal at www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Zero Sensitive Category Targeting: Inwoovation Lab never combines third-party advertising cookies with sensitive personal data, farm financial records, or proprietary facility coordinates.

8. Statutory Data Subject Rights Under GDPR (EEA/UK), CCPA/CPRA & PIPA

Depending on your jurisdiction of residence, you hold enforceable statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom & Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): Pursuant to Articles 15–22 of the GDPR, you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), the right to data portability, the right to restrict processing, and the right to withdraw cookie consent at any time via our Consent Management Platform without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (e.g., BfDI/BayLDA in Germany).
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, California residents have the right to know what categories of personal information are collected, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Inwoovation Lab does not sell personal information for monetary consideration, and never knowingly collects data from minors under 16 years of age.
  • Client-Side Web Storage Transparency: Interactive tools (such as the AgriMaster BOM Builder, VenloCAD 3D project presets, and Quiz certificate generator) store user-entered parameters exclusively in your browser's local window.localStorage or volatile memory. No calculation inputs are uploaded to external databases.

9. Data Retention, Edge Security & Policy Updates

All traffic to inwoovation.com is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 through Cloudflare's global edge infrastructure. Ephemeral security logs used to mitigate automated botnet or DDoS traffic are automatically purged within 30 days. Material updates to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page with an updated effective timestamp.
