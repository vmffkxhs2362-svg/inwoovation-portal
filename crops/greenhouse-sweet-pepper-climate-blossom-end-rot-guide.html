🌡️ Microclimate Dynamics & Temperature Steering (DIF)

Maintaining a DIF of +5 to +8°C (Day Temperature minus Night Temperature) regulates internode length and crop balance. When vegetative vigor is excessive, reducing DIF steers the plant toward reproductive flowering; increasing DIF restores leaf expansion and canopy Light Extinction (Beer-Lambert k-factor).

Target root-zone drain fraction: 25 - 35% to avoid substrate EC accumulation and root hypoxia.

🐞 Biological Pest Management (IPM) & Key Pathogens

Primary Pathological Risks: Blossom End Rot, Flower/Fruit Abortion, Sunscald, Thrips (Frankliniella occidentalis), Pepper Mottle Virus.

Biological Predator Sizing & Release Strategy: Amblyseius swirskii (50–100 mites/m²), Orius laevigatus (0.5–1.5 bugs/m²), Phytoseiulus persimilis.

❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

❓ How do you prevent flower abortion in sweet peppers?

Ensure night temperatures do not drop below 16°C or exceed 20°C. Maintain a 24-hour mean temperature (24h-T) between 20.5°C and 22.0°C and avoid extreme VPD peaks (>1.6 kPa) that trigger stomatal closure.
