Hydroponic Greenhouse Sweet Basil & Culinary Herbs (Ocimum basilicum)

Commercial greenhouse basil requires high temperatures (never below 15°C to avoid chilling injury) and moderate VPD. Managing night humidity and air circulation is the single most critical intervention to prevent catastrophic Basil Downy Mildew (Peronospora belbahrii) epidemics.

🌡️ Microclimate Dynamics & Temperature Steering (DIF)

Maintaining a DIF of +4 to +6°C (Day Temperature minus Night Temperature) regulates internode length and crop balance. When vegetative vigor is excessive, reducing DIF steers the plant toward reproductive flowering; increasing DIF restores leaf expansion and canopy Light Extinction (Beer-Lambert k-factor).

Target root-zone drain fraction: NFT Recirculating to avoid substrate EC accumulation and root hypoxia.

🐞 Biological Pest Management (IPM) & Key Pathogens

Primary Pathological Risks: Basil Downy Mildew (Peronospora belbahrii), Chilling Injury (<12°C), Fusarium Wilt, Fungus Gnats.

Biological Predator Sizing & Release Strategy: Steinernema feltiae nematodes, Amblyseius swirskii, Bacillus amyloliquefaciens.

❓ Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

❓ Why is night temperature critical for basil?

Basil is of tropical origin and suffers severe physiological chilling injury when exposed to temperatures below 12°C. Cold foliage also condensates dew, instantly triggering Basil Downy Mildew sporulation.
