🔬 Errata & Peer-Review Submission Guidelines

If you identify an edge-case anomaly or wish to challenge a computational result, please include the following technical details in your submission to expedite verification:

  • Exact Input Parameters: Air temperature, relative humidity, external wind speed, radiation level, or pipe diameter used during the session.
  • Expected vs. Observed Values: The numerical value generated by our simulator versus your measured field data or engineering table benchmark.
  • Standard Reference: If applicable, cite the relevant DIN, ASABE, FAO, or peer-reviewed literature source establishing the expected baseline.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I request a custom calculation tool for my specific crop or facility?

Yes. We continuously expand our suite based on community demand. Please submit your requested equations and crop physiological parameters through our Inquiry Form. High-impact tools for commercial CEA are prioritized for release in bi-weekly sprint updates.

How do I report a security vulnerability or website bug?

Please email contact@inwoovation.com with the subject line [SECURITY DISCLOSURE]. We acknowledge reports within 24 hours and practice coordinated vulnerability disclosure.

Can university courses and agricultural extension agents embed these tools?

Yes. We actively encourage educational use in agricultural engineering, horticulture, and plant science courses. You are free to link directly to any tool, embed simulation widgets in teaching syllabi, or cite our biophysical equations in academic publications under open-access principles.

What documentation should accompany a new standard proposal?

Please provide the formal DOI or standard designation number (e.g., ISO, DIN, ASABE, or Wageningen UR technical report), the governing mathematical formulation, and a sample validation dataset containing benchmark input-output test pairs.
