Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 08, 2026

At AgriMaster (Inwoovation Lab), available from https://inwoovation.com/parts, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by AgriMaster and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

AgriMaster is designed as a client-side serverless application. We do not maintain user databases or store your personal identity information on centralized servers.

  • Browser Local Storage: Filter parameters or preferences may be temporarily cached in your web browser for usability.
  • Geolocation (Optional): If you use the 50km nearby dealer lookup feature, location data is processed locally within your browser using standard HTML5 Geolocation APIs to calculate distance to dealers. No GPS coordinates are stored on our servers.

2. Cookies and Analytics

We utilize industry-standard analytics tools to monitor website performance and improve user experience:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Collects anonymous usage statistics such as page views, device type, and referral sources.
  • Google AdSense: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Third-Party Links & Procurement Services

AgriMaster provides direct links to third-party marketplaces (Amazon, AliExpress) and local equipment dealers for component procurement. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us via our Feedback & Inquiry Form.
