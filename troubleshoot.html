TOMATO

Blossom End Rot (BER) & Apical Calcium Necrosis

Root Cause: Insufficient xylem mass-flow transport of calcium (Ca²⁺) to expanding distal fruit tissue caused by excessive daytime VPD (>1.6 kPa), elevated K⁺/NH₄⁺ antagonism, or sub-20% drain cycles.

🩺 3-Step Corrective Protocol:
1. Steer midday greenhouse VPD into 0.75–1.05 kPa via high-pressure fogging.
2. Maintain A/B feed Ca²⁺ ≥ 4.5 mmol/L and keep NH₄⁺ < 8% of total nitrogen.
3. Maintain 0.5–1.0 cm root-pressure hydration overnight to preserve cell wall pectate integrity.

Thin Apical Heads & Weak Flower Trusses (RTR Imbalance)

Root Cause: 24-hour mean temperature exceeds the assimilate supply generated by ambient Daily Light Integral (excess nocturnal dark respiration vs. low DLI).

🩺 3-Step Corrective Protocol:
1. Lower target 24h mean temperature by 1.5–2.0 °C to restore the Radiation-to-Temperature Ratio (RTR).
2. Run a -3 °C pre-night drop for 2 hours after sunset to redirect carbohydrates into fruiting trusses.
3. Prune 1 older lower leaf per week or activate supplemental LED toplighting.

🩺 Field Diagnostic Reference Manual: Symptom-to-Biophysics Matrix

In commercial greenhouses and vertical farms, visible crop stress is almost always the downstream manifestation of an upstream psychrometric, aerodynamic, or ionic mass-balance failure. Applying chemical fungicides to Botrytis cinerea without correcting nocturnal dew-point crossing, or spraying foliar calcium onto lettuce heads without breaking the stagnant meristem boundary layer, treats only the symptom while the physical root cause persists. 🔬 The 3-Layer Root-Cause Audit Protocol (Meister Standard)

Whenever an anomaly appears in a commercial bay, execute this sequential audit before altering fertilizer recipes or spraying crop protection agents:

  1. Verify Sensor Calibration & Aspirated Placement: Check whether the climate computer's Pt1000/capacitive RH box is aspirated (fan running at 3 m/s) and positioned at the active apical meristem height. An unshielded sensor in direct sunlight reads 3–5 °C too high, tricking the computer into over-ventilating and inducing severe midday VPD stress.
  2. Inspect Daily Drain Volume, EC & pH Differential: Compare drip inflow EC/pH against slab leachate EC/pH every morning 3 hours after sunrise. If drain EC exceeds inflow EC by more than +1.2 mS/cm while drain percentage falls below 18%, the crop is under osmotic water stress regardless of aerial humidity.
  3. Audit Nocturnal Screen & Pipe Transients: Overlay 5-minute time-series graphs of thermal screen position, rail pipe temperature, and indoor RH during the 2 hours following sunrise. Sudden screen retraction drops cold attic air (10 °C) onto warm foliage (19 °C), causing instantaneous thermal shock and condensation.

❓ Greenhouse Troubleshooting Frequently Asked Questions

How can greenhouse growers distinguish calcium Blossom End Rot from fungal rot?

Blossom End Rot (BER) starts strictly at the dry, sunken, leathery blossom scar (distal apex) of rapidly expanding green fruit without surface mycelium. It is caused by inadequate xylem calcium transport under excessive VPD (>1.6 kPa) or salt stress rather than a primary pathogen.

Why does inner-leaf tipburn occur in vertical farms even when calcium feed exceeds 180 ppm?

Calcium travels almost exclusively in the xylem transpiration stream rather than phloem sap. Stagnant air (<0.15 m/s) traps a saturated boundary layer inside enclosed lettuce hearts, halting xylem flow into newly unfolded leaves regardless of reservoir concentration.

When should Sodium (Na⁺) trigger recirculation drain bleed in rockwool greenhouses?

Crops exclude most sodium ions at the root membrane, causing recirculated drain Na⁺ to climb over weeks. Salt-sensitive strawberries require bleed above 1.5–2.5 mmol/L, whereas greenhouse tomatoes tolerate up to 8.0–10.0 mmol/L Na⁺ before osmotic yield penalty sets in.

How much heating fuel is wasted by unsealed thermal screen edge gaps?

Because warm greenhouse air rises via buoyancy convection through open screen perimeter seams, leaving just 1% to 2% linear edge gap reduces true thermal energy retention by 25% to 35% on winter nights under DIN V 18599 heat balance modeling.
