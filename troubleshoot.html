🩺 Field Diagnostic Reference Manual: Symptom-to-Biophysics Matrix

In commercial greenhouses and vertical farms, visible crop stress is almost always the downstream manifestation of an upstream psychrometric, aerodynamic, or ionic mass-balance failure. Applying chemical fungicides to Botrytis cinerea without correcting nocturnal dew-point crossing, or spraying foliar calcium onto lettuce heads without breaking the stagnant meristem boundary layer, treats only the symptom while the physical root cause persists. Use the comprehensive diagnostic reference matrix below alongside the interactive cards above to audit your facility:

Distal Fruit Blossom-End Rot (BER)
Tomato, Sweet Pepper, Eggplant		 Foliar transpiration monopolizes xylem water flux during high radiation spikes; high slab salinity or NH₄⁺ blocks root Ca²⁺ uptake. Midday VPD > 1.45 kPa; Slab EC > 4.8 mS/cm; Solar > 750 W/m² Deploy diffuse shade screen > 650 W/m²; mist to hold VPD 0.85–1.15 kPa; keep night RH 80–84% for root guttation Ca²⁺ push.
Inner Heart Apical Tipburn
Vertical Farm Lettuce, Strawberry Calyx		 Thick laminar boundary layer ($r_b$) over enclosed meristem suppresses local transpiration despite abundant root-zone Ca²⁺. Canopy airspeed < 0.15 m/s; DLI > 17 mol/m²/d; Night VPD > 0.8 kPa Direct 0.35–0.55 m/s downward/horizontal airflow into heart; provide 3–4 hr humid night window (RH > 85%) for guttation transport.
Nocturnal Dew & Botrytis Stem/Bud Rot
Cannabis, Rose, Tomato, Cucumber		 Rapid post-sunset air cooling or cold pre-dawn fruit thermal mass drops surface temperature ($T_s$) below indoor air dew point ($T_{dp}$). $T_{ ext{surface}} - T_{ ext{dewpoint}} < 1.0 ext{ °C}$; Night VPD < 0.30 kPa (RH > 88%) Run minimum heating pipe (40–45 °C) 45 min before sunrise & sunset with 2–5% leeward vent crack or mechanical dehumidifier.
Rhizosphere pH Crash (< 5.0) & Root Pythium
Rockwool, Cocopeat, NFT Channels		 Excessive $ ext{NH}_4^+$ cation uptake releases $ ext{H}^+$ protons into slab; warm stagnant solution (>25 °C) depletes dissolved oxygen. Drain pH < 5.1; $ ext{NH}_4^+ : ext{NO}_3^- > 12\%$; Dissolved $ ext{O}_2 < 5.5 ext{ mg/L}$ Reduce $ ext{NH}_4^+$ below 5% of total N; buffer feed with 0.8 mmol/L $ ext{HCO}_3^-$; chill nutrient tank to 19–21 °C ($DO \ge 8.0 ext{ mg/L}$).
Closed-Loop Sodium ($ ext{Na}^+$) Lockout
Recirculating Gutter Systems		 Selective root exclusion of $ ext{Na}^+$ and $ ext{Cl}^-$ concentrates ballast ions in recycled leachate, raising non-nutritive osmotic EC. Drain $ ext{Na}^+ > 6.0 ext{ mmol/L}$ (Strawberry/Rose) or $> 10.0 ext{ mmol/L}$ (Tomato) Blend rainwater/RO permeate ($EC < 0.1 ext{ mS/cm}$) into makeup water and execute partial leachate discharge when $ ext{Na}^+$ ceiling hits.

🔬 The 3-Layer Root-Cause Audit Protocol (Meister Standard)

Whenever an anomaly appears in a commercial bay, execute this sequential audit before altering fertilizer recipes or spraying crop protection agents:

  1. Verify Sensor Calibration & Aspirated Placement: Check whether the climate computer's Pt1000/capacitive RH box is aspirated (fan running at 3 m/s) and positioned at the active apical meristem height. An unshielded sensor in direct sunlight reads $3 ext{–}5 ext{ °C}$ too high, tricking the computer into over-ventilating and inducing severe midday VPD stress.
  2. Inspect Daily Drain Volume, EC & pH Differential: Compare drip inflow EC/pH against slab leachate EC/pH every morning 3 hours after sunrise. If drain EC exceeds inflow EC by more than $+1.2 ext{ mS/cm}$ while drain percentage falls below $18\%$, the crop is under osmotic water stress regardless of aerial humidity.
  3. Audit Nocturnal Screen & Pipe Transients: Overlay 5-minute time-series graphs of thermal screen position, rail pipe temperature, and indoor RH during the 2 hours following sunrise. Sudden screen retraction drops cold attic air ($10 ext{ °C}$) onto warm foliage ($19 ext{ °C}$), causing instantaneous thermal shock and condensation.
