📚 OpenCEA Biophysics Study Guide & Core Formula Compendium

Before or after attempting the 30-question multi-track certification assessment above, review the five foundational biophysical and thermodynamic pillars used by commercial greenhouse engineers and German Gärtnermeister crop managers:

1. Tetens Saturation Vapor Pressure & Leaf-to-Air VPD

Unlike simplified room-air VPD charts that assume leaf temperature equals ambient dry-bulb air temperature (Ta), true stomatal transpiration driving force depends on the leaf internal saturation vapor pressure (es(TL)) measured via infrared radiometry minus ambient air vapor pressure (ea = es(Ta) × RH/100):

e_s(T) = 0.61078 × exp[ (17.27 × T) / (T + 237.3) ] kPa   |   VPD_leaf = e_s(T_leaf) - [ e_s(T_air) × (RH / 100) ]

When a densely transpiring tomato or cucumber canopy cools its leaves 1.5–2.5 °C below ambient air temperature via latent heat of vaporization (λ = 2.45 MJ/kg), true VPDleaf is 0.25–0.40 kPa lower than air VPD. Maintaining VPDleaf within 0.65–1.15 kPa maximizes stomatal conductance (gs) for CO2 diffusion without triggering abscisic acid (ABA) midday stomatal closure.

2. Daily Light Integral (DLI), Photomorphogenesis & FvCB Photosynthesis

Instantaneous Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density (PPFD in μmol·m-2·s-1, 400–700 nm) integrates over the photoperiod (H hours) into Daily Light Integral: DLI = PPFD × H × 0.0036 mol·m-2·d-1. Under the Farquhar-von Caemmerer-Berry (FvCB) C3 biochemical model, enriching indoor CO2 from 420 ppm to 850–1,000 ppm suppresses Rubisco oxygenation (photorespiration) and raises the light-saturated net assimilation rate (Amax) by 25–38%. Adding 5–10% Far-Red photons (730 nm) lowers the Phytochrome Photostationary State (PSS = Pfr/Ptotal), inducing petiole expansion and driving the Emerson enhancement effect across Photosystems I and II.
