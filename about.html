About Inwoovation Lab

We build free, open-access biophysical simulation tools that help commercial greenhouse growers, vertical farmers, and agricultural engineers make data-driven decisions — anywhere in the world.

🎯 Our Mission

Complex horticultural engineering — VPD microclimate control, photosynthetic light optimization, fertigation chemistry, energy modeling — has traditionally required expensive proprietary software or deep academic training.

Inwoovation Lab breaks down these barriers by creating browser-based, zero-install engineering calculators that run instantly on any device. Every tool is free, requires no registration, and works offline.

⚡ Technology Philosophy

Every tool is built with zero external framework dependencies — pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. This means:

  • Instant loading — No npm, no bundler, no build step. Sub-second page loads worldwide.
  • Works offline — Once loaded, tools function without an internet connection.
  • 100% client-side privacy — No data ever leaves your browser. Zero telemetry on calculations.
  • Runs anywhere — Desktop, tablet, or mobile phone in a greenhouse with spotty WiFi.

📐 Governing Physical Equations & Engineering Validation Charter

Every calculator, monograph, and crop steering atlas published by Inwoovation Lab is governed by strict deterministic biophysics rather than empirical rules of thumb. Before deployment, our computational kernels are verified against our automated 20-unit biophysics regression suite:

  • Evapotranspiration & Canopy Microclimate (FAO-56 / Stanghellini Penman-Monteith) — Full coupling of net isothermal radiation (Rn), aerodynamic boundary-layer resistance (ra), and light/VPD-modulated stomatal resistance (rs), incorporating radiometric leaf-to-air temperature offsets (ΔTleaf) via the Magnus-Tetens saturation vapor pressure curve.
  • Photosynthetic Carbon Assimilation (Farquhar-von Caemmerer-Berry C3 Model) — Mechanistic calculation of Rubisco carboxylation capacity (Vcmax), RuBP regeneration electron transport rate (Jmax), and Arrhenius temperature kinetics for supplemental CO2 enrichment optimization.
  • Hydroponic Ion Speciation & Electroneutrality (Davies & Extended Debye-Hückel) — Activity coefficient corrections for divalent cations (Ca2+, Mg2+) and sulfate/phosphate complexation in closed-loop rockwool and cocopeat fertigation systems, paired with Van Genuchten substrate matric potential curves.
  • Greenhouse Thermodynamics & Structural Mechanics (DIN V 18599 & EN 13031-1) — Hourly dynamic heating/cooling balance, multi-layer aluminized thermal screen U-value reduction, Mollier h-x psychrometric dehumidification state transitions, and Eurocode wind/snow structural truss verification.
