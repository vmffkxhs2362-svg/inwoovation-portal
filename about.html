About Inwoovation Lab

We build free, open-access biophysical simulation tools that help commercial greenhouse growers, vertical farmers, and agricultural engineers make data-driven decisions — anywhere in the world.

🎯 Our Mission

Complex horticultural engineering — VPD microclimate control, photosynthetic light optimization, fertigation chemistry, energy modeling — has traditionally required expensive proprietary software or deep academic training.

Inwoovation Lab breaks down these barriers by creating browser-based, zero-install engineering calculators that run instantly on any device. Every tool is free, requires no registration, and works offline.

While our simulation tools remain 100% open-access, Inwoovation Lab provides bespoke cross-border engineering audits and technical advisory for commercial greenhouse developers, institutional agtech funds, and equipment manufacturers:

  • DIN 11535 & DüV Compliance Audits — Formal review of closed-loop fertigation drain, nitrogen runoff caps, and European greenhouse structural integrity.
  • HVAC & ATES Feasibility Verification — Independent third-party review of geothermal heat pump sizing, Mollier psychrometrics, and peak electrical demand.
  • 50-State USDA Grant Technical Submission Packages — End-to-end preparation of bank-grade engineering feasibility narratives for REAP ($250k–$500k) and EQIP capital.
  • Cross-Border Technology Transfer — Bridging German Gärtnermeister precision standards, Dutch Venlo automation, and North American commercial scale.

🔬 Core Research Areas

  • Greenhouse Climate Physics — VPD, psychrometric Mollier analysis, thermal screen heat loss, natural ventilation convection modeling
  • Photobiology & Spectrum Engineering — DLI optimization, PAR/Far-Red canopy extinction (Beer-Lambert), LED junction thermal design, photomorphogenesis
  • Precision Fertigation & Hydroponics — Nutrient solution formulation, chemical speciation (Debye-Hückel), rockwool moisture/drain optimization
  • AI & Autonomous Control — MPC reinforcement-learning climate controllers, MQTT IoT fleet simulation, digital twin modeling
  • Energy & Structural Engineering — CHP flue gas SCR, thermal buffer tank stratification, RO desalination, wind/snow load (EN 13031)
  • Crop Science & Plant Health — Farquhar photosynthesis (FvCB), crop steering (generative/vegetative), transpiration-calcium-BER risk, biological IPM predator sizing

⚡ Technology Philosophy

Every tool is built with zero external framework dependencies — pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. This means:

  • Instant loading — No npm, no bundler, no build step. Sub-second page loads worldwide.
  • Works offline — Once loaded, tools function without an internet connection.
  • 100% client-side privacy — No data ever leaves your browser. Zero telemetry on calculations.
  • Runs anywhere — Desktop, tablet, or mobile phone in a greenhouse with spotty WiFi.

🌱 Open Access Commitment

Inwoovation Lab believes that precision agriculture knowledge should not be locked behind paywalls. Our tools are free for every grower on earth — from a small-scale farmer in Kenya to a 100-hectare Venlo glasshouse operator in the Netherlands.

All technical articles include academic citations (DOI/PMID) and are available in English, German, and Japanese to maximize global accessibility.
