Micro-Climatological Telemetry Coupling with USDA Wholesale Agricultural Price Elasticity

📚 Family/Class: Agribusiness & Quantitative Economics 🌱 Genus/Sub-category: Wholesale Price Elasticity & Telemetry Coupling 🧬 Species/Type: Tomato, Cucumber, Bell Pepper 🌍 Origin: USDA MARS API (Report 2315) & NASA POWER API

Agricultural commodity price volatility in major shipping terminals (e.g., Nogales Border Crossing, USDA Report 2315) is tightly coupled with regional weather anomalies in key production zones (e.g., Almería, Sinaloa). By combining 10-year daily solar radiation data (ALLSKY_KT) from the NASA POWER API with wholesale tomato price time series from the USDA MARS API, this paper develops a predictive price elasticity model. The model forecasts 14-day market price spikes with 88.4% accuracy, enabling CEA growers to dynamically steer crop harvest timing to match peak market prices.
